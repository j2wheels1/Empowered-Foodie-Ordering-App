Add Our Ordering App to Your Phone

Takes 30 seconds. No app store needed.

Open the Ordering App

iPhone / iPad (use Safari)

  1. Open the ordering app in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and tap “Add to Home Screen”.
  4. Tap “Add”. The Empowered Foodie icon now sits on your home screen.

Android (use Chrome)

  1. Open the ordering app in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots menu in the top right corner.
  3. Tap “Add to Home screen” (or “Install app”).
  4. Tap “Add”. The Empowered Foodie icon now sits on your home screen.

Placing your order

  1. Open the app from your home screen and enter the email address we have on file for you.
  2. Choose your dishes and servings, add any notes, and tap Submit.
  3. You will receive a confirmation email right away.
